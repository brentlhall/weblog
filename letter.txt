Letter
======
June 25, 2026

I received a letter in the mail, yesterday.
*
It was from an old friend of mine from school. We've been exchanging letters for a few years now. 
*
I think exchanging letters using the mail is an excellent way to communicate. It is far slower than digital communication but when writing a letter, you have far more opportinity for thought. The ability to think before exchanging is important to me. I can scratch out words or lines and even throw my letter in the trash and start over. Of course, some of this is true for email and text but I'm far less likely to edit these forms of communication.
*
My friend and I send these letters because we have something to talk about. It could be family related, or something that happened, or what we're reading. Often it's simply a thought on some subject we wish to share.
*
Our letter writing is not scheduled. We write whenever the mood strike us.
*
I initiated this letter writing a few years ago by sending letters to a large number of family and friends. I was hoping for several participants. Most of them replied with a phone call, or a text, or email. So far only one has consistantly continued to write and I cherish his letters when ever they arrive.
*
I now realize why my parents and grandparents were so fond of the letters they received.
*
My only wish would be that the postal service would drop the price of personal mail and raise the price of the junk mail I receive daily. I'm using forever stamps I purchased several years ago but the price of a stamp for a letter has risen yearly. Mailing a letter now costs around eighty cents. This is most likely a huge deterent to personal letter writing.
*
Check my info if you'd like to mail me a response.
