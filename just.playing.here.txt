Just Playing Here
=================
November 2, 2026

I'm writing this on my Amazon 10" tablet using my finger as a stylus! Gboard Handwrite makes this possible but I'm sure there are other apps that would work just as well.

My largest problem with blogging or writing in general, using a digital device, seems to be using a keyboard. Though I've been using devices with keyboards for many years, I've never become adept in their use and I'm less than efficient at moving thought onto media with them.
*
I do function better on larger keyboards but they still tend to stand in the way.
*
In the past, I've learned to use my phone's stylus in place of typing and I really liked the results but my tablets don't have a built in stylus. I never dreamed that using my finger would work so well!
*
Just playing at something can often lead to better function.
*
I've always figured that a desktop or laptop would be best for writing but now I'm definitely leaning toward my tablets and phone. I hope this leads to many more additions to my blogs and notes!
